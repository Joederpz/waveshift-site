Three steps

Record. Rebuild. Export.

01

Play your set

Plug your laptop into the same switch as your CDJs and mixer and press Record. WaveShift joins Pro DJ Link and listens over USB MIDI, logging loads, tempo, playhead, faders, EQ, filters and effects. Or skip the laptop and let the WaveShift Recorder do it.

02

Rebuild it

Plug the CDJ USB stick into your laptop and WaveShift finds the tracks you played, or point it at your music folder or a rekordbox XML. Your set is rebuilt from your own mastering-grade files.

03

Fix it and export

Trim, move, split, re-blend. Redraw an EQ curve, swap an effect, ramp the tempo. Check the loudness, then export a 16 or 24-bit WAV ready for SoundCloud or a mix series.

Straight answers

Does it record audio?

No. It records control data: which track was loaded, when it played, the tempo, the faders, EQ and filter moves, and the mixer's effects. The audio is rebuilt from your own files, which is why your tracks stay mastering-grade.

Will the export sound exactly like the club?

It is a faithful rebuild, not a copy. The EQ, filter and effects are close approximations of Pioneer's, and the preview and the export use the same maths so what you hear is what you get.

How does it find my tracks?

Record with your CDJ USB stick in the decks, then plug it into your laptop and WaveShift reads the track list from it. Or give it your music folder or a rekordbox XML export.

What happens when the trial ends?

Recording and export stop until you enter a key. Opening and editing your sets keeps working, so you never lose access to them.

Does it phone home?

No. The editor is bundled and only talks to itself on your own machine. The one exception is a once-a-day check for a new version, which you can switch off in settings.

Is it affiliated with Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta?

No. WaveShift is independent software that is compatible with their gear.
